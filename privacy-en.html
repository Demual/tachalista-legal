Privacy Policy

Tachalista app (com.jrsoft.listacompra) · Last updated: 29 October 2026

In one sentence: Tachalista keeps your shopping lists on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers of its own, and your lists never leave your phone unless you share them. Apart from that, the only data that leaves it is the technical and advertising data collected by Google's ads library, as explained below.

Who is responsible

The data controller is JR Soft, trading name of Javier Román Sáez, developer of the Tachalista app. You can get in touch at demual@gmail.com.

What this app does NOT do

  • There is no sign-up, account, username or password for the app. You are never asked for an email address, a phone number, a name or your location.
  • It does not upload your lists. Your lists, the items on them with their prices and notes, the aisles and your shopping history are stored on your phone. This app has no servers of its own.
  • The app includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics and no Crashlytics). Google's ads library does send its technical data, explained below.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. To show and measure ads, Google's ads SDK (AdMob) collects device identifiers, as explained in this policy. Some US privacy laws may treat that as “selling” or “sharing” personal data (see “US residents”).
  • It does not ask for permission to use the microphone, the camera, your files, your contacts or your location. Dictation is handled by the phone's own voice service, and files are opened and saved through Android's screens, only the ones you choose.
  • Your lists are not included in Android's backup to Google's cloud.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state and advertising identifier (AD_ID and the permissions of Android's advertising services), which the advertising needs (see below).
  • The ones Google's libraries add on their own (WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE), which give no access to any of your data. Keeping the screen on in store mode needs no permission.

Data that stays only on your phone

This information is kept in the app's private storage. It disappears if you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings.

  • Your lists: their name, their budget and their items, with the quantity, unit, price, aisle, note and whether each one is already in the cart.
  • The aisles and their order, including the ones you create or rename.
  • The product memory (what you usually buy, which aisle you put it in and its last price), used for autocomplete.
  • The history of finished shopping trips, with their items and total.
  • Your settings: language, theme, currency, store mode text size and the other options.

Backups and switching phones. Android's cloud backup (the one in your Google account) is turned off for this app's data: none of it is uploaded. Android does keep a list of the apps you had installed in your account so it can reinstall them, but Google manages that list and it contains no data from this app.

If, while setting up a new phone, you copy your data directly from the old one, with or without a cable, the app's data may be moved to the new phone. You ask for this transfer and Android carries it out, using its own tool or your phone maker's, under their own terms. According to Android's documentation, it goes from one phone to the other without going through servers. It never goes through ours: JR Soft receives nothing.

Whether it happens depends on your Android version and phone maker (it doesn't happen on Android 8 or earlier), so we can't guarantee it. If you set up the new phone from your Google account backup without the old phone, this app's data won't come back, because it isn't in that backup. So you don't have to rely on that, you can export it yourself (see below). Your ad choices are not transferred: the app will ask you again on the new phone.

What you choose to share

  • Sharing a list. “Send the list” prepares a text with the items and a link, and hands it to the app you choose on Android's screen (messaging, email…). “Copy the link” puts it on the clipboard and “Show the QR code” displays it on screen so another phone can scan it. The list travels inside the link itself: anyone who has the link or the QR code can see the list, so only share it with people you want to, and don't put anything on a list that you wouldn't want its recipients to see.
  • Opening a list link. The links start with https://demual.github.io/l/. If the phone has Tachalista, they open in the app. If not, they open in the browser on a page that shows the list. That page is served by GitHub Pages, which receives the IP address and the usual data of any visit to a website, under its own policy. The list goes in the part of the link after the “#”, which browsers don't send to the server: the page reads it inside the browser. JR Soft receives nothing, and the page has no analytics and no cookies.
  • Receiving a list or a text. A list someone sends you, or a text you share with Tachalista from another app, is read on your phone and is only saved if you decide so.
  • Clipboard. The app only reads the clipboard when you tap “Paste from clipboard”, and only writes to it when you tap “Copy the link”.
  • Dictation. The microphone button opens the phone's speech recognition (usually Google's). What you say is processed by that service, under its own terms, and the app only receives the recognized text. Tachalista does not record or store audio.
  • Export and import. “Export my lists” saves a file with everything wherever you choose, through Android's screen. If that folder syncs with a cloud (for example, Google Drive), the syncing is done by the service you chose, under its own terms. “Import a copy” reads the file you choose and adds whatever is missing, without deleting anything.
  • Write to us. It opens your email app with a message addressed to us, with no data about you. It is only sent if you send it. If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you tell us; we use them only to reply to you, and we delete the message once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after receiving it.

Data that does leave your phone

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app is free and is supported by unobtrusive Google AdMob ads, which only appear on the lists screen and in the history: never on an open list, in store mode or in full screen. Ad requests carry nothing from your lists.

As with any app that uses AdMob, Google's SDK collects the Android advertising identifier (you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads), the app set identifier (app set ID), your IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred), your interactions with the app and with the ads, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data to serve and measure advertising, for statistics and to prevent fraud, under its own policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before serving personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If you decline, you will see non-personalized advertising (or limited advertising, without identifiers, if you also decline the use of device storage) and the app works exactly the same.

Google shows the ad consent message with wording that is common to all its advertising partners, which is why it mentions precise geolocation. According to Google, its ads SDK only uses the approximate location derived from your IP address, and this app does not give it your GPS location. You can refuse that use in “Manage options”.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ratings and Google Play

Every now and then, after you finish a shopping trip, the app may ask you for a rating through Google Play's official window, which is managed by Google. “Rate the app” and “More apps by JR Soft” open Google Play. To show you only the apps available in your country, “More apps by JR Soft” checks, on the phone, the country of the SIM card or of the system region; that information does not leave your phone. Its links to Google Play carry a parameter that only says they come from Tachalista, with no data about you.

IP address

As with any app that connects to the internet, the Google services mentioned above receive your IP address when connecting. This app has no servers of its own, so it keeps no logs of any kind.

Legal basis and retention

Legal basis. What is processed on your phone does not reach JR Soft: your lists are stored so that the app can do what you ask it to (Art. 6.1.b GDPR). For what does leave it: (1) personalized advertising and the use of device storage for advertising purposes in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: your consent, which you give or refuse in Google's form and can withdraw in Settings → Ad privacy options (Art. 6.1.a GDPR and Art. 22.2 LSSI-CE); (2) non-personalized advertising, its measurement, technical logs and fraud prevention: the legitimate interest in funding a free app, keeping it working properly and protecting it from fraud (Art. 6.1.f GDPR); (3) if you write to us: the legitimate interest in replying to you and solving what you tell us about (Art. 6.1.f GDPR). Google processes the AdMob data as an independent controller, under its own policies.

Data stored on your phone remains there until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data. This app keeps no data outside your phone, except the messages you send us, which are deleted once the matter is resolved and, at the latest, one year after we receive them; Google keeps its own data according to its policies.

Your rights

If you live in the European Union, the GDPR grants you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. In practice, this app keeps none of your data outside your phone except the messages you send us (for those, write to the contact address); AdMob data is processed by Google, with whom you can exercise your rights (policies.google.com/privacy). For what is on your phone:

  • Access and portability: share a list or export everything to a file from Settings → Export my lists.
  • Rectification: change any item, list or aisle in the app itself.
  • Erasure: delete an item, a list or a shopping trip from the history, or clear the app's data from Android settings; you can also uninstall it. There is no need to ask anyone.
  • Objection and withdrawal of consent: change your advertising choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

You can also write to the contact address or lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos) or with the data protection authority of your country.

US residents

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to ask that your personal data not be sold or shared for targeted advertising. You can do this in the app: Settings → Ad privacy options → “Don’t sell or share my data”. From then on, you will only see non-personalized ads. The app does not send sensitive data to anyone; your lists stay on your phone. For ads, Google's SDK uses device identifiers and an approximate location (city level). For any other request about your data, write to us at demual@gmail.com.

Children

Tachalista is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors; the technical data sent by AdMob is the data described above.
